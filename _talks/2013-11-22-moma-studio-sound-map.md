Presented with Laura Beiles. In the summer of 2012 we built an online sound map and asked the public to contribute field recordings from their lives, as the sound component of the MoMA Studio: Common Senses space held in conjunction with the *Century of the Child* exhibition. The map grew to over a hundred recordings - wind turbines in Andalucía and Ireland, chanting from Japan, a manifestation in Madrid, streets in London, a soundwalk through the Museum itself.
