Presented with Shannon Darrough. In July 2013 MoMA launched a rebuilt version of the MoMA App, created to replace the handheld audio wands distributed in the museum. The talk covered the app's features and design, an overview of the new system's architecture, the design collaboration with MoMA's Education department, and the agile process the development team ran with partners at Pivotal Labs.
