Hello there! 

I'm a technology leader with 20+ years helping cultural institutions transform their digital presence and organizational practices. 

At [The Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://www.metmuseum.org), my work has spanned mobile development, API architecture, contributing to the institution's [Open Access](https://www.metmuseum.org/about-the-met/policies-and-documents/open-access) initiative, and currently leading web modernization efforts &mdash; building sustainable systems and collaborative teams throughout.

Before joining the Met, I worked in Digital at the Museum of Modern Art (MoMA), where I contributed to the development of [moma.org](https://www.moma.org), the native MoMA Audio+ app, and hands-on gallery experiences.

My background combines field recording and sound design with a long-standing practice in coding. I hold a master's from NYU's Interactive Telecommunications Program (ITP), where curiosity and experimentation were central. That experience taught me the value of creating environments where people can explore ideas without fear of failure &mdash; an approach I bring to building both user-centered systems and collaborative teams.

My work focuses on sustainable technical practices, championing open-source solutions, and creating collaborative environments where both technical excellence and professional growth thrive.
