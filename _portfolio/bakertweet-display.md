A scrolling marquee in the gallery, fed by the bakers downstairs.

[BakerTweet](https://www.moma.org/interactives/exhibitions/2011/talktome/objects/146207/), designed by Poke, is a metal box with a dial, a button, and a small screen.  A baker turns the dial to whatever just came out of the oven and presses the button, and the device posts it.  One was installed in MoMA's Cafe 2 for the run of the show, posting as @MoMABakerTweet.

I polled the RSS feed the work generated and displayed it in the gallery as a scrolling marquee, so each fresh batch was announced upstairs as it happened.

Shown in [*Talk to Me: Design and the Communication between People and Objects*](https://www.moma.org/calendar/exhibitions/1071), July 24 – November 7, 2011, organized by Paola Antonelli with Kate Carmody.
