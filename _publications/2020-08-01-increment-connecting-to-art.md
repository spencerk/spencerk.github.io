How a three-person team built The Met Collection API, starting from a private API for the collection object page and the nightly ETL that already fed the website. Google Arts & Culture was the first partner. The article covers why the API was left open with no authentication, how the data was linked to Getty vocabularies and Wikidata, and what happened when people outside the museum started using it, including a Kaggle competition that drew over 500 teams.
